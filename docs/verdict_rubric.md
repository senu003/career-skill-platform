# Final Verdict Rubric

This rubric defines the expected final verdict for a candidate evaluation independent of any machine learning model or specific programmatic threshold logic.

## 1. Interview Ready
**Definition**: The candidate meets or exceeds the core expectations of the role and presents a strong case for an immediate interview.
**Criteria**:
- The candidate possesses all (or nearly all) of the **Required** skills.
- The candidate's assessed proficiency levels are equal to or greater than the required proficiency levels for the vast majority of skills.
- Any missing skills are either marked as **Preferred** (nice-to-have) or are auxiliary to the core role.
- Assessment performance is generally strong (e.g., scores > 70%).

## 2. Short-Term Prep
**Definition**: The candidate has a solid foundation but exhibits specific, addressable gaps that require a short period of focused preparation.
**Criteria**:
- The candidate possesses most of the **Required** skills but may be missing 1 or 2.
- The candidate may have a proficiency gap (e.g., they have Basic knowledge but Intermediate is required) in a few core areas.
- The candidate may need to "Upgrade" or "Speed Practice" certain skills.
- Assessment performance is mixed (e.g., scores between 40% and 70%).

## 3. Major Upskill Required
**Definition**: The candidate lacks the fundamental qualifications necessary to be successful in the role in the near term.
**Criteria**:
- The candidate is missing a significant portion (e.g., > 40%) of the **Required** skills.
- The candidate has severe proficiency gaps across multiple core skills (e.g., Assessed as Basic when Advanced is required, or Re-learn Basics is recommended).
- The candidate performed poorly across multiple key assessments (e.g., scores < 40%).
- Substantial time and effort would be required to bridge the gap to the role's requirements.
## Tie-break rules (added after the first evaluation run)
A required skill counts as FAILED if any of these is true:
- it is missing from the CV and has no assessment,
- its assessment score is below 0.40,
- its assessed level is two or more levels below the required level.

Verdict by share of required skills that FAILED:
- 0% to 20%  -> Interview Ready (and no failed skill is marked Required-critical)
- more than 20% up to 40% -> Short-Term Prep
- more than 40% -> Major Upskill Required
